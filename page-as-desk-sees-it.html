The three problems

  1. Parser swaps day and month. The shared parser reads dates month first; every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. All channels. Ticket: https://github.com/SrulyAI/mega-what-it-shows/issues/1.
  2. booking-v3 hint asks for the month first. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says and the form reads it day first. Form channel at booking-v3 tenants only. The parser fix does not touch it. Ticket: https://github.com/SrulyAI/mega-what-it-shows/issues/2.
  3. Dates move by one day. No swap: the date lands a day early or late, and the same meeting shows different days in different cities. Listed in the desk’s own notes as a one-day move but filed under the parser. Cause not confirmed; it looks like time zones. Ticket: https://github.com/SrulyAI/mega-what-it-shows/issues/3.

Why they were merged

Triage merges on subject line alone (decision 0001). The three problems share subject lines (“Wrong day on the invite” turns up in all three), so the subject rule cannot tell them apart. Only the body, the tenant and the channel can.

Lessons

  • Count reports by cause, not by subject. A big ticket can hide more than one bug.
  • Read a sample of bodies before marking a merged ticket known.
